How it works

Three steps. Mostly one.

Trigger it

Press Alt+Shift+P (configurable) or click the toolbar icon. That's the whole interaction.

It captures

The page scrolls itself, waits for each section to render, and stitches everything into one image.

Edit & share

Crop, annotate, hide private bits, then download as PNG/JPEG/PDF or copy and paste it anywhere.

Private by design

Your screenshots never leave your device

No tracking, no ads, no remote servers. The extension installs with a minimal permission set; broad access is requested on demand and only to capture cross-origin iframes.
